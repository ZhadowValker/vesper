Vesper privacy

Vesper is a planning tool. This page says what happens to your information when you sign in with Google.

What Vesper keeps

  • Your Google email address, so it knows which projects are yours.
  • The projects, tasks and notes you write in Vesper.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: a sign-in permission that lets Vesper add events you ask for to the calendar you pick.

Where it lives

Your projects are stored on the server that runs this Vesper, kept apart from everyone else's. A copy is also saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Vesper can open. Vesper does not read any other file in your Drive, and does not read your calendar events.

What Vesper does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data, and shows no ads.
  • It does not use your notes to train any model.
  • If you use the AI helper with your own key, the text of the note you ask about goes to the AI service you chose. Nothing is sent unless you press an AI button.

Your choices

  • Delete my data in the Sync dialog removes your data from the server and your Google Drive copy, and signs you out.
  • You can also remove Vesper's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
