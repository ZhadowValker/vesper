Vesper privacy

Vesper is a planning tool. This page says what happens to your information when you sign in with Google.

What Vesper keeps

  • Your Google email address, so it knows which projects are yours.
  • Your projects, tasks and notes, which stay in your own Google Drive and are not stored on this server.
  • Calendar permission, asked together with sign-in: lets Vesper add events you ask for to the calendar you pick. You can untick it on Google's screen.

Where it lives

Your projects, tasks and notes are saved only in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Vesper can open. This server does not keep a copy. While you sync, it passes your data between your device and your Drive and does not store it. The server keeps only your email address, a sign-in session, and the access key Google gives it so it can reach that folder (and your calendar, if you allowed it). The person who runs this Vesper keeps their own projects on the server. Vesper does not read any other file in your Drive, and does not read your calendar events.

What Vesper does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data, and shows no ads.
  • It does not use your notes to train any model.
  • If you use the AI helper with your own key, the text of the note you ask about goes to the AI service you chose. Nothing is sent unless you press an AI button.

Your choices

  • Delete my data in the Sync dialog removes your Vesper file from your Google Drive and your sign-in details from the server, and signs you out.
  • You can also remove Vesper's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
